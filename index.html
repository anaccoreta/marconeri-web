ABOUT

Marco Neri does not believe in reality anymore.

He believes in art.

His spiritual-magical thinking has taken him to explore far beyond the musical scores to deep into a unique musical approach.

He seeks to motivate all to enjoy the ecstasy of sound.

Bio — Composer | Sound Artist

I am sound creator, producer, and mentor Mexico-born based in Canada. My work explores sound from an interdisciplinary perspective, often combining performers' physical gestures with the acoustic and spatial impact of their movements. This approach frequently results in hybrid performances that merge staging, live projection, and sonic experimentation, allowing me to explore conceptual ideas through movement and sound.

I'm particularly interested in offering alternatives to traditional concert music formats. My work aims to engage curious audiences who are open to new sonic experiences, including those unfamiliar with contemporary music. I seek to connect with emerging new music listeners, interdisciplinary artists, and younger generations through immersive, exploratory performances that bridge individuality, sound, and technology.

My musical journey began in childhood when I studied piano before expanding into drums, flute, guitars, and synthesizers. Over the years, I've collaborated with contemporary dance companies as a composer, touring internationally across Europe and Latin America. I've also created music for theatre, avant-garde performances, and film and have recently begun exploring sound installations.

I specialize in sound creation using computer programs such as Max MSP, Unity, in combination with movement interfaces like Arduino, Mugic sensors, and movement tracking systems. This allows me to integrate a performer's physical gestures into sound generation and visuals control, developing a unique approach to concert music and performance.

In 2016, I founded a music label, Ajusco Recording Studio, where I have produced and released over 50 concert music recordings by emerging and established musicians. I also co-founded the Onepanko Youth Orquestra, where I worked as a composer, producer, and conductor.

Through all of these projects, I aim to bridge artistic disciplines and foster meaningful connections with diverse audiences and communities. Living in Canada has shaped my approach to collaboration and experimentation, offering access to a wide vision of cultural perspectives, community, and diversity.

Automaton-Music

Apr. 28 2022 - 5:00pm (EST)

This piece examines the association between movement and sound through the concepts of counterpoint and control.

Counterpoint unites the temporal aspect of music with movement. It is a route to witness the transformations of an entity, whether visual or acoustic, over time. One of the performers plays an acoustic violin, and the other makes the actions of playing the violin without the instrument.

This second performer holds a wireless device called Mugic connected to a computer that triggers and manipulates the electronic sounds.

Mugic is an interface that translates movement into computer language.
